Approach

Assess. Engineer. Implement. Improve.

The same four moves every time. What changes is the discipline, and the licensed signatory the work ends up needing.

Assess

Walk the facility or read the project. Establish the baseline, the legal trigger, and which licence the deliverable actually requires.

Engineer

Size the intervention against real numbers — load, cost, throughput, payback. No recommendation survives if it fails on capital cost.

Implement

Build it, or hand over drawings a contractor can actually construct. Support through installation and commissioning.

Improve

Measure what happened against what we predicted. Feed the gap back into the next cycle.

Accreditations

Licensed and verified

Energy audits are delivered by our own EPRA-licensed audit team. EIA and environmental audit reports are signed by our in-house NEMA Lead Expert. Design and construction drawings are issued under the seal of the registered professional engineer assigned to the work.

We will give you the registration numbers on request, and we would encourage you to check them against the regulator's register before you appoint us. Every regulated deliverable also carries the name and registration number of the professional who signed it.

On energy audit obligations

Kenya's energy audit obligation has been in flux. Coverage turns on annual electricity consumption — the current threshold sits at 180,000 kWh — and facilities are expected to implement a minimum share of the savings identified. We confirm the applicable cycle and threshold for your specific facility before quoting, rather than repeating a figure that may be out of date. Use the threshold check above for an indicative position on your own consumption.

Who signs the work

Our EPRA-licensed energy audit team signs statutory and voluntary audits. Our in-house NEMA Lead Expert signs EIA and environmental audit reports. For design and construction drawings, we engage the registered professional engineer the work requires — they review, seal, and sign the drawings, and their name and EBK registration number appear on the document. Every regulated deliverable is signed by the certified professional whose licence applies to it.
